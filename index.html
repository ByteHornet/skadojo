Welcome to SKA DOJO

More Than Karate. A Way of Life.

Kyokushin Karate training can support discipline, perseverance, physical fitness, confidence, respect and personal growth. Whether you are stepping onto the mats for the first time or returning after years of practice, you are welcome here.

SKA DOJO is a place to train hard, learn properly and be part of a community that supports every student.

  • Discipline Focus built through repetition.
  • Strength Physical fitness and resilience.
  • Confidence Grow on and off the mats.
  • Respect For teachers, partners and the dojo.
